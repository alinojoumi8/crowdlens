Private pilot notice

This pilot is local, invitation-only and limited to fictional test data. Do not upload legal-case material, personal information or confidential documents.

The operator can access data on the local host. Uploaded material, extracted text, graphs, simulations, reports, reviews and backups may remain until the operator performs a coordinated deletion. Removing a project does not promise deletion from every store or backup. Contact the person who invited you for access, deletion or support requests.

When the operator explicitly authorizes a model run, selected source text and prompts are sent to the configured MiniMax service. No Canadian processing location, zero retention or no-training promise has been established for that provider. Embeddings remain local through Ollama. There is no new authorization to generate merely by signing in.

The identity provider receives your sign-in and manages passwords, MFA and recovery. CrowdLens stores its issuer/subject identifier, verified email, role, invitation records and revocable sessions locally. No identity-provider access or refresh tokens are retained by CrowdLens.

Generated activity and analysis are exploratory, require review and are not validated forecasts or professional advice. Use only data you are permitted to provide. Do not attempt to access other users’ material or bypass limits. External hosting and external tester project access remain disabled pending separate readiness decisions.
