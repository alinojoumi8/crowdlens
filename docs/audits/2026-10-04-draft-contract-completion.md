## Behavior

Every new proposal requires exactly one explicit model source judgment per ledger claim. Missing, duplicate, unknown-source, unsupported, ambiguous, conflicting or unverified judgments block. Analyses also require supported premise judgments. These judgments are fallible AI opinions, not factual certification. Instructions preserve recorded absence, procurement status, population definitions and possible conditional support without strengthening them.

The summary audit must judge the resulting summary even when edits exist. New producers return `summary_consistent`; the legacy Boolean remains supported, but missing/nonboolean/conflicting aliases block. New draft producers return one `section_summary` text and its claim links, or null. The server owns offsets and replaces only a unique verbatim occurrence of the selected section in the frozen summary. All other characters remain protected. Unmapped abstract summaries remain unchanged for review. No-op targets still undergo claim validation. Legacy offset/replacement producers remain compatible under strict scope and claim validation. Acceptance recomputes scope from the sealed parent for both new policies; old proposal digests are unchanged.

A bounded two-word title-topic query complements title, description and comparison-section queries. A separate reservoir-fencing development regression proves retrieval despite a generic title suffix and excludes foreign-case evidence. Scope/version filters, no-anchor behavior and source bounds remain intact.

Review exports retain original documents/filenames, original comparison summaries, resulting summaries, raw section targets and normalized edits. Cases without a usable draft are not represented as reviewable candidates.
